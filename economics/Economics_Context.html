⚡ Quiz yourself

The quiz uses the cards in your current filter above (0 cards). Narrow it with Micro/Macro, a category or "Not yet" to target weak spots.

🕵️ Mystery Context starter

A real-world example is shown with the concept blanked out. The class names the concept, then you reveal it and the key figures. Uses your current filter.

🎯 Present one card

One slide that builds step by step: concept → what happened → key figures → evaluation → "tell me why" questions.

🎲 Cold-call spinner

Picks a random concept from the filter and asks a "tell me why" question. Students answer verbally, then you reveal the model point.

📂 Present by category

Each category runs as its own slide sequence: a title slide, then one build-up slide per card. Press Space or → to reveal the next step, and Esc to exit.
