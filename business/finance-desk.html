The Finance Desk

You own the business and you need money. Walk the finance district, fill in the applications, sit the interviews and negotiate the deals. Then build a finance package, defend it to your board under pressure, and write up your decision.

Stage 2 · The finance district

Visit every office

Each office gives you an offer, a refusal or a reason it doesn't apply. Everything you are offered goes into your deal folder.

Stage 3 · Finance package

Build your package

Not all money is the same. Cash raised is new money into the business. Cash released is money already tied up in the business that you free up. Payment deferred is a bill you pay later: it still has to be paid.

Your finance package

Only sources with an offer can be used. Divisible sources let you take part of the offer; the others are all or nothing.
Stage 4 · Board challenge

Defend your package

The board has read your package. They will test your reasoning with "what if" questions. Choose your response, then finish the sentence in your own words. You can defend your decision or go back and revise it.
