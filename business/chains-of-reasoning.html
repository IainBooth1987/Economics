💡 Planning scaffold
1
Know it (AO1): Identify the one or two concepts the question is really about and show you understand them — briefly, not a glossary dump.
2
Use it (AO2): Apply to the type of business or situation in the question (e.g. a plc, a healthcare producer) with realistic examples. If a sentence would fit any business at all, sharpen it.
3
Chain it (AO3): Build two developed chains: cause → effect → effect → consequence for the business. Two deep chains beat four shallow points.
4
No verdict needed: "Analyse" does not reward a final judgement or conclusion — spend the time deepening the chains instead.
"This means that…" "…because…" "As a result…" "This leads to…" "For [business], this is significant because…" "In turn…"

What this question assesses
AO1 knowledge · AO2 application · AO3 analysis. There is no AO4 credit in an Analyse question, so a conclusion or judgement earns nothing extra. Marks come from the quality and development of the chains, not the number of points.
