Which specification this is for

This tool is built for the new AQA Business specification — AS 7137 / A-level 7138 — first taught from September 2026, with AS exams from 2027 and A-level exams from 2028. It is not the current 7131/7132 specification that most cohorts are still sitting through 2026. The two specs share a subject but not always the same structure, wording, or unit numbering — if your class is on the outgoing 7131/7132 course, this bank won't line up cleanly with your scheme of work.

What this is

A pit stop is a four-question retrieval starter, run at the start of a lesson. Unlike a normal recap, it's built on interleaved retrieval practice: instead of only reviewing what you just taught, you deliberately reach back to several earlier points in the course — a topic from last lesson, last week, last month, and last year — in the same four minutes.

Retrieving spaced-out material is harder than reviewing what's fresh, and that difficulty is the point: it's what makes the memory stick and builds the connections across the specification that the extended-response questions depend on.

Why you build it yourself

The four slots always mean the same thing — last lesson, last week, last month, last year — but which question fills each one is up to you. Every school sequences its scheme of work differently, so pick whichever topic from the bank actually was covered last lesson, last week, etc. for your class.

The four circuits

Mapped to the accredited AQA (7138) paper structure: Unit 1 covers what is business, marketing and financial management; Unit 2 covers people and operations management; Unit 3 (A-level only) covers business and society, the external environment, and strategy. AQA builds synoptic linkage into Papers 2 and 3 by design — Paper 2 draws on Unit 1's finance/marketing content, and Paper 3 draws on both Units 1 and 2 — so the Synoptic circuit deliberately links a decision in one unit to its consequence in another, e.g. an HR decision that shows up in a financial ratio.

Running it

Build the pit stop, then hit Present to put it on the board full-screen. Students answer in black pen; click a card to reveal the model answer and mark corrections in purple.
