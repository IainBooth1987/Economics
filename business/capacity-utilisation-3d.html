What can the manager do?

If utilisation is too low

  • Promote off-peak deals or cut prices on quiet nights.
  • Find new customers, such as conferences or business travellers.
  • Rationalise: close or sell spare capacity, such as a floor.
  • Rent spare space to other businesses.

If it is close to 100% too often

  • Increase capacity, for example by building an extension.
  • Subcontract: send overflow guests to a partner hotel.
  • Raise prices at peak times to manage demand.
  • Plan maintenance and refurbishment for quieter weeks.
The Harbour Hotel and its costs are fictional. Room price £140 a night; variable cost £30 per occupied room (cleaning, laundry, toiletries, energy).
