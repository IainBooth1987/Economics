Finance & Accounts

Understanding Dividends

A two-tab resource on dividends: what they are, why they incentivise shareholders, and why some PLCs choose not to pay them and still attract demand. Tab 1 covers the dividend per share and dividend yield formulas with worked examples, live calculators and six practice questions with a printable answer key. Tab 2 is "The Income Investor Challenge" — a interactive simulation where students invest £10,000 for income, react to news alerts that move share price and dividend per share, decide whether to hold or sell & switch, and must justify each decision in writing before it unlocks.
