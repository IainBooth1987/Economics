What is BIZ-OMICS?

BIZ-OMICS is a hub of resources for A-Level Economics and Business, built by a practising teacher and exam board examiner — for students studying towards AQA, Edexcel, OCR and WJEC specifications.

Alongside free tools like diagram flashcards, key term glossaries, adaptive practice questions, arcade games and monthly news updates covering key business and economic stories, the shop offers premium simulated experiences and digital textbooks.

It's built to do more than just prep you for exams — it's here to build genuine understanding and get you enjoying economics and business along the way.

Got a question, spotted an error, or fancy getting in touch? Use the contact form — we'd love to hear from you.
